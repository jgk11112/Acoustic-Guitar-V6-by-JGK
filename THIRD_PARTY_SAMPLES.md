# Third-party sample provenance

## Martin HD28 steel-string acoustic guitar

- Instrument: 2017 Martin HD28 Vintage Series
- Recording / author: Jeff Learman
- Upstream project: `sfzinstruments/Discord-SFZ-GM-Bank`
- Instrument: `026-Acoustic Guitar (steel)`
- Licence: Creative Commons CC0 / public domain dedication
- Pinned upstream commit: `05d5ed8befa042fd9d99a6d159dfc3673d3f8edc`

The GitHub build downloads 15 mapped WAV recordings from that pinned commit and
embeds them into the VST3. The installed plugin therefore does not need an
internet connection or a separate sample folder.

The project name/UI do not claim endorsement by Martin or any artist/manufacturer.
